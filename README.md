# Building a Self-Funding Economy with AI and Blockchain

Our project explores how **AI, blockchain, and digital consumption** can work together to build a self-funding economy.

## 1. Interblockchain Swaps

Enable users to transact across different blockchains through a unified wallet experience.

- **Proof of Concept:** Dogecoin → Solana
- **Status:** Built
- **Link:** [https://ranchimall.github.io/solanawallet/#/buy]

## 2. Self-Funding Metrics

A self-funding economy can grow through two primary mechanisms:

1. **Recurring Cash Flow** — Generate a regular flow of revenue to fund ongoing operations and development.
2. **Growing Attention → Growing Consumption** — Attract increasing attention to products and services, leading to greater consumption and economic activity.

### Sources of Attention

Growing attention can come from:

- **A Single Product:** One successful product attracts users and drives consumption.
- **A Portfolio of Products:** Multiple products, applications, and content channels attract different audiences and create multiple sources of consumption.

### What We Measure

The goal is to understand how attention translates into consumption, how consumption generates value, and how that value can support further development.

## 3. AI Apps

Build AI-powered applications that create digital products and generate value through usage and consumption.

- **Proof of Concept:** Music AI
- **Status:** Built

Music AI explores a decentralized music library on FLO Blockchain, featuring human- and AI-written lyrics, AI-generated music, and value driven by consumption.

## Objectives of Frontiers 2026

Parts 1 (**Interblockchain Swaps**) and 3 (**AI Apps**) were developed independently before Frontiers 2026.

Our primary objective at Frontiers 2026 is to build a **Proof of Concept (PoC) for Part 2: Self-Funding Mechanisms**, demonstrating how blockchain applications, AI-powered products, and growing consumption can contribute to a self-funding economic system.

## The Vision

**AI creates. Blockchain coordinates. Consumption generates value.**

Together, these components explore a self-funding economic system in which digital products attract attention, consumption generates economic activity, and the resulting value supports continued creation and growth.
